# Tawala – Data Policy

**Effective Date:** 9 August 2026  
**Last Updated:** 9 August 2026

This Data Policy explains how Tawala handles Customer Data within our Business Management System and clarifies the responsibilities of both Tawala and our customers under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

---

## 1. Roles and Responsibilities

| Party       | Role                          | Responsibilities |
|-------------|-------------------------------|------------------|
| Customer    | Data Controller               | Determines the purpose and means of processing personal data of their own customers and staff |
| Tawala      | Data Processor                | Processes Customer Data only on documented instructions from the Customer |
| Tawala      | Data Controller               | For platform account data, billing data, and usage analytics |

---

## 2. Categories of Data Processed

We process the following categories of data on behalf of Customers:

- Identity and contact data of the Customer’s staff and end-customers
- Transaction and sales data
- Inventory and product data
- Invoicing and payment records
- System logs and audit trails

---

## 3. Processing Instructions

Tawala will only process Customer Data:
- To provide the Service
- To maintain security and prevent abuse
- To comply with applicable law
- As otherwise documented in the Customer’s instructions or configuration within the platform

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures, including:

- Encryption of data in transit
- Access controls and role-based permissions
- Regular backups
- Audit logging of privileged actions
- Secure password and PIN storage (hashed and salted)

---

## 5. Sub-processors

We may engage sub-processors (such as cloud hosting providers and payment processors) to support the delivery of the Service. We remain responsible for their compliance with data protection obligations.

A list of current sub-processors can be provided upon request.

---

## 6. Data Subject Rights

Where a data subject (e.g. a Customer’s end-customer or staff member) exercises their rights under the Data Protection Act, the Customer (as Data Controller) is primarily responsible for responding.

Tawala will provide reasonable assistance to the Customer in fulfilling such requests.

---

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

- Active data is retained for the duration of the Customer’s subscription.
- Upon termination or deletion request, Customer Data will be made available for export for thirty (30) days.
- Thereafter, personal data will be deleted or irreversibly anonymised, except where retention is required by Kenyan law (particularly tax and accounting records).

---

## 8. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach affecting Customer Data, we will:

- Notify the Customer without undue delay
- Provide sufficient information to allow the Customer to meet their own notification obligations under the Data Protection Act, 2019

---

## 9. International Transfers

Where Customer Data is transferred outside Kenya, we ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2019.

---

## 10. Audits and Compliance

Upon reasonable written request, and subject to confidentiality obligations, we will provide information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Data Policy and applicable data protection laws.
